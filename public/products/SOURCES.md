# Product photography sources

Real-world product photos for the SD Computers demo catalog (40 products
across 10 categories). Each file lives at `public/products/<slug>.jpg` and
is referenced by `products.image` in the database (and by
`backend/sql/seed.sql`, so a fresh reseed resolves the same path).

Images were located via image search, downloaded and normalised to a
900px, white-background JPEG. They remain the copyright of their
respective owners (manufacturers / retailers) and are used here for
this academic demo only — do not ship them commercially without
clearing rights.
